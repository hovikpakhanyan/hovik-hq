# Hovik’s Investment Plan

Last updated: November 11, 2026 (Pacific). This is a research and planning document, not trade advice, and no trades are placed from it.

## Goal

Hovik is 34 and wants to grow his investment account toward a couple of million dollars — to set up his kids and to serve as a family financial tool. This is a long-horizon growth plan, not a short-term income plan.

## Allocation: 60 / 40

- **60% core sleeve.** Well-performing quality names: established, liquid companies and broad funds meant to be held for years. This is the foundation of the account.
- **40% high-upside / higher-risk sleeve.** Asymmetric bets, where the potential upside is large relative to the amount risked. This sleeve can lose money, including most or all of a position.

This is a target mix, not a daily mandate. The account will not sit at exactly 60/40 every day. Suggested default classifications (to be confirmed as holdings are reviewed): broad funds such as VOO, VYM, and QQQ, and quality large-caps, lean core; leveraged funds such as TQQQ, and speculative names such as RGTI, lean to the 40% sleeve. Overlap matters: QQQ and TQQQ already hold names such as NVDA, AMD, and GOOGL, so those bets can stack.

## Paper trading

The paper-trading (play-money) sleeve lives inside the 40% risk bucket. Simulated trades are rehearsals for real money in that same sleeve — same kinds of names, same rules — not a separate strategy. There is no live brokerage execution. A starting virtual balance has not been set yet and should not be invented.

## Morning research

Every weekday, a research scan starts at 5:30 AM Pacific (as early as 5:00, never later than 5:30) so the report is in the Grok Bot stock-research chat by 6:00 AM, well before 8:00 AM. It covers public activity on X, major news outlets, and global trends. Scope is the whole board — what’s trading, what’s moving, and upcoming IPOs — not only the current watchlist. Holdings, names being tracked, and research candidates stay in scope and are prioritized when something material hits them.

Each alert is tagged against this 60-40 plan:

- **Core fit** — belongs in the 60% quality sleeve
- **Upside fit** — belongs in the 40% high-upside sleeve (including paper-trade ideas)
- **Noise** — worth knowing, but not an action for either sleeve

Reports are a fast rundown: one bold headline takeaway per story, one short line of context, and a source link for more detail.

These weekday research alerts are delivered in the Grok Bot stock-research chat only, and only when something materially new matters. They are not auto-published elsewhere until a secure publishing path is in place.

## Quarterly rebalance

At the start of each quarter (first Monday of January, April, July, and October), check whether winners in the 40% sleeve have quietly grown that bucket past its target, and whether the 60% core has drifted. This is a check and a recommendation, not an automatic trade.
